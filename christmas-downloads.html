📌 How to Make Your Card

  • Download the front cards — choose red back or white back (or both!). Open the PDF and select only the page you want to print.
  • Download an inside template — open in Adobe Reader, click inside the text box and type your personal message or names. Leave any text box empty if you don't need it.
  • Print the front on cardboard or heavier paper (up to 175GSM on a home printer). Print the inside template on standard paper.
  • Assemble — place the inside template back-to-back with the front card and add a small spot of glue to hold them together.
  • Fold in half — your finished A5 card (14.8 x 21cm) is ready to send! 🎄
